## 1. What EVEOps Is

EVEOps is a free, open-source, unofficial third-party tool. It is not a hosted service — there is no EVEOps account, no EVEOps-operated backend, and no EVEOps-run database of user data. The App runs locally on your Mac and communicates directly with:

- CCP Games' EVE Swagger Interface (ESI) and EVE SSO, using your own EVE Online credentials via OAuth 2.0 + PKCE;
- optional third-party data services you choose to enable (e.g. zKillboard, Janice, Fuzzwork, EVE Scout);
- optionally, the Discord desktop client on your own Mac (for Rich Presence) and/or a Discord webhook URL you provide (for alert notifications).

## 4. Discord Integration

EVEOps offers two optional Discord features, both of which you must explicitly enable:

- **Webhook alerts** — sends a copy of your in-app notifications (e.g. skill queue, structure, contract alerts) to a Discord channel webhook URL that you create and provide. EVEOps does not have access to your Discord account, server, or channel beyond that single webhook endpoint.
- **Rich Presence** — while enabled, shows your currently selected character's ship and system as your Discord activity status, via a direct local connection to the Discord desktop client running on your own Mac. This requires no login or authorization to your Discord account.

Both features can be turned off at any time in EVEOps' Settings, which immediately stops any further data from being sent.
