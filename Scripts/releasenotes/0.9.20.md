Route Planner: What's at stake? --> Before a lowsec trip, see what a loss would cost you: your ship, fit and cargo, the implants in your pod, and what Platinum insurance would pay back. If your implants are valuable, it tells you whether you have an implant-free jump clone to leave them behind with.
Route Planner: Can you escape a gate camp? --> See how fast your current ship aligns and warps with your skills, implants and fit, and a plain verdict: warps cloaked, hard to catch, catchable by fast lockers, or catchable. When it can be caught, you get a tip to fix it, like the MWD + cloak trick or a scout for haulers.
Settings: How accurate are the briefs? --> Settings > Advanced shows how often the Route Planner's lowsec briefs were right, checked against kills in the 30 minutes after each one. Everything stays on your Mac.
