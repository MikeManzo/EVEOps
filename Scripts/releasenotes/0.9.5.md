- One-time manual update: EVEOps now signs its updates with a new key, so please download 0.9.5 from the release page and replace your current copy in Applications. Automatic updates work again from 0.9.5 on.
- Training Overview has been completely redesigned. A new header shows your pilot, total SP and skill level breakdown, your attributes and implants, and your progress toward mastering every skill group — next to a live "Now Training" ring with countdown, SP per hour, queued SP and what's up next.
- Training now has Queue and Skills tabs. The Skills tab lists every skill you know in a sortable table, with search and a skill group filter.
- Training also suggests an attribute remap when one would finish your queue sooner, shows your saved skill plan, and tracks your next SP milestone and what a large skill injector is worth at your SP.
- Skill Planner: Import, Copy and Clear now live in the toolbar, each planned skill shows when it will finish, and you can right-click a skill to change its target level, move it, or remove it.
- Market Browser, Galaxy Market Search and the Fitting Shop now use native Mac tables — resize and sort columns, select several rows, copy with ⌘C, and double-click an order or station to set it as your autopilot destination.
- Right-click almost anything — pilots, corporations, alliances, factions, systems, items and skills — to copy its name or ID, search the market, set a destination, or open it in zKillboard, EVE Who, DOTLAN or EVE Ref.
- Detail panels now open in a resizable inspector on the right side of the window.
- Search fields look and work the same everywhere, and ⌘F jumps to the search field on any screen.
- Screens that are loading show placeholder rows instead of a spinner, and the smallest text across the app is larger and easier to read.
- Galaxy Market Search, Trade Hub, Skill Tree and 3D ship windows now remember where you put them, and Esc closes them.
- Discord Rich Presence now tells you when your Discord app doesn't support it (for example Swiftcord) and lists the clients that do.
- Fixed: the Remap Advisor mixed up implant bonuses (an Intelligence implant was counted as Memory), which could skew its advice.
- Fixed: untrained skill levels were invisible in Light mode.
- About now credits DOTLAN, EVE Who, EVE University and Discord.
