- **3-D Galaxy Map** — the Galaxy Map gains a rotatable, zoomable 3-D star map alongside the existing 2-D view (toggle in the toolbar). Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, ⌥-drag to pan.
   - Every k-space solar system (~5,500) and stargate connection (~7,000) is rendered, not just constellations — pulled back you see ~800 constellation-level dots for a clean overview; click one, or just zoom in, to drop down to individual systems with name labels.
   - Four colour modes shared with the 2-D map: **Region**, **Space** (high/low/null-sec + Pochven), **Security**, and **Kills**.
   - Click any system for a detail panel — security, region/constellation, recent kill activity — with one-click **View Constellation Map**, **Set Destination**, and **Plan Route**. Clicking also lights up that whole region's gate network while dimming the rest, so local structure is easy to trace.
   - **Find Me** zooms straight to your current system, marked with a small pulsing "you are here" ring that stays a consistent size at any zoom level.
   - A Gates toggle hides the stargate web when you just want to look at the systems.
