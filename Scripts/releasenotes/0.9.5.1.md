- Fixes
   * Training Overview no longer hangs or crashes on the Trained Skills tab. Clicking Trained Skills could freeze the app with a spinning cursor and then quit it. Switching between Queue and Trained Skills now works reliably.
Switching Training Overview tabs is now instant. Pilots with long skill queues no longer get a pause of several seconds the first time they switch between Queue and Trained Skills.

- Improvements
   * Training Overview opens faster. EVEOps now loads your attributes, implants and skill-group progress in the background when it starts. The header shows up complete instead of filling in piece by piece after you open the screen.
