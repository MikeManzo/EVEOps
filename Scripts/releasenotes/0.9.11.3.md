- Exploration Codex: a new Universe screen for Ghost Sites (all four Covert Research Facility tiers) and Sleeper Caches (Limited / Standard / Superior). Pick a site on the left and a resizable click-to-inspect pane on the right.  Lays out its security band, defenses, mechanics, severity-graded hazards, tactics and recommended ships. Loot values are cross-referenced live so the reference is always current.
   - Readiness check: reads the signed-in character's Hacking / Archaeology / Astrometrics levels and active implants and gives each site a Ready / Marginal / Under-skilled verdict, with specific advice ("train Hacking to V", "fit a virus-coherence implant"). The verdict also shows on every row in the list.
   - Ghost Site response timer: start it the moment you land; it counts down the response-fleet window and fires a notification (plus an optional Discord ping) at the 30-second warning and at zero.
   - Blast survivability: enter your worst-case EHP and get a Survivable / Risky / Lethal call against each tier's failed-hack explosion (≈1k in high-sec up to ≈7.6k in wormhole space).
   - Live loot values: every drop is priced from Jita daily history, with an indicative consumables floor and the jackpot rolls flagged separately.
   - Market signal: each site's drops are scored against their 30-day median (Spiking / Elevated / In line / Soft) so you know when it's worth selling, with per-item change badges and a price-history chart for the headline item.
   - Run ledger: log a haul after a run (tick what dropped, valued live at Jita, optional duration); tracks run count, total ISK, best run and ISK/hour per site, stored on-device.
   - Scouting: a live "is it hot where I am" read for your current system, alongside a jump to the Galaxy Map's Kills mode.
