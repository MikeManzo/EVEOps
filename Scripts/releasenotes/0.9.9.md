- **Login Planner (new):** Works out the fewest logins that keep every pilot's training, industry, PI and market orders running. Set how long things may sit idle and the hours you can play. Each login lists who to log in and what's due, how many logins you save compared with answering every timer, and what skipping one would cost.
- **Dead Stock (new):** Finds what you own but never use — anything not in a ship, not a blueprint, not an industry material, and not needed by any pilot's saved fittings. Each item is priced at Jita (sell now and if listed), rated for how quickly it would sell, and shown with where it sits. "Copy for Appraisal" copies the list for Janice, Evepraisal or EVE's multi-sell. EVEOps now records how long items sit untouched, so that column fills in over the coming weeks.
- **Hangar Matrix (new):** Every saved fit from every pilot, checked against every pilot. Each cell shows how close that pilot is to undocking and what's in the way: jumps, ISK or training. Pilot cards show location, ship, SP, jump clones and how many fits each pilot is ready for; click one to sort the matrix for that pilot. Each fit shows its value, contents and who saved it, and clicking a cell shows parts, training and CPU/powergrid details.
- **Skill ROI (new):** Ranks skills by what they unlock for you per day of training — saved fits that become flyable, fits that get closer, and more industry, market, PI or clone slots where yours are busy. "Copy for EVE" copies your picks as a skill plan you can paste into the in-game skill queue.
