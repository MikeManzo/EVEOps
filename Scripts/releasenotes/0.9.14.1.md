Ready Room counts the Tech II gear you already own: a Hammerhead II in your drone bay, or a T2 module on your ship, now covers a fit that calls for the Tech I version, as long as your skills can use it. Fits no longer ask you to buy parts you've already upgraded past. 
Buy lines show what you're upgrading from: each part to buy shows what's in that slot on your ship today, or that the slot is empty. It also shows how much the purchase improves the fit, such as +8% EHP or +12% DPS. Hover for the before → after numbers.
